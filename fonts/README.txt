Fonts used by livingroomlabs.in: Inter, JetBrains Mono, Playfair Display and Space Grotesk.
All four are open-source fonts published on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). They are served from this folder instead of fonts.googleapis.com
so visitors' browsers don't need a connection to Google before text can be shown.
