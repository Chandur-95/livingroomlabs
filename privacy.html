Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Living Room Labs is a photography and film collective based in Hyderabad, India. This page explains what information this website collects and why. If anything here is unclear, write to us at hello@livingroomlabs.in.

When you send us an enquiry

The enquiry form (and booking through our chat helper, Fotu) asks for your name, your email address, the kind of shoot and the details you choose to write. This reaches our inbox through Formspree, the service that delivers our website forms. We use it only to reply to you and to plan your shoot. We don't sell it, and we don't add you to mailing lists.

When you chat with Fotu

Fotu answers your questions inside your browser. To improve its answers, the site keeps anonymous counts: how often Fotu is opened, which topics come up, which page it was on and whether you were on a phone or a computer. It also keeps the text of questions Fotu couldn't answer, with email addresses and phone numbers removed automatically. None of this is linked to your name or to who you are.

Settings saved in your browser

The site remembers a few small choices on your own device, such as light or dark mode, whether you've already seen the opening screen, and whether you've hidden Fotu. They stay on your device and are never sent to us. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep things

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any booking that follows, and for any records we have to keep. Fotu's daily counts are deleted automatically after about 400 days, and only the most recent unanswered questions are kept.

Photos of people on this site

If you appear in a photo on this site and would like it taken down, email us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
